# No test splits unmechanized from judgment

- **What.** A row moves to `judgment` on the wording of its reason. Five rows that read the same stay `unmechanized`.
- **Where.** `lint/rule_mechanisms.json`.
- **Decision.** `claude-md-shrink` (CLAUDE.md drops the prose of a rule that a guard or gate enforces).
- **Retire.** A stated test in the decision decides each row, and the five rows follow it.
