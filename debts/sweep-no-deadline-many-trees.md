# The session-end sweep's deadline does not bound a many-tree pass

- **What.** The SessionEnd deadline does not bound a pass over many trees. Idle time is read for every tree before any tree is judged. The husk and trash scans check the deadline only at their start. The per-root setup has no deadline check. Review round 3 measured at 150 trees: `find_trash` and `find_husks` took 13.3 s, and idle reads took 7.3 s of the 12 s budget. Idle reads can run up to about 12-20 s before a decision, against about 1-3 s on main. Dirty, unpushed, live-session and process checks still keep a tree. A call that starts before the deadline still runs to its own timeout. Per-call timeouts: `guard._git` 10 s, `ls-remote` 10 s per remote, `rev-list --stdin` 30 s, a repo's `reap:worktrees` script 15 s. The budget is fixed at 12 s. `session_end_sweep.py` kills the sweep at min(25 s, the time its hook has left), which can be under 12 s. So a finish inside the kill limit is not proven. A kill during `git worktree remove` can leave a half-removed tree. How often each happens is unmeasured. Named in review round 2 of lane sweep-fast, item (d), and in review round 3, items 1, 2 and 4.
- **Where.** In `janitor/sweep.py`: `_oldest_idle_first` (idle read for every tree before any decision), `find_husks` and `find_trash` (deadline checked at entry only), `prune_registrations` and `resolve_default_base` as called from `sweep_repo` (no deadline check), and `JUDGE_BUDGET_SECONDS` with `main` (the fixed budget). In `hooks/guard_core.py`: `_git`. In `janitor/session_end_sweep.py`: the sweep call and its kill limit.
- **Decision.** `agent-end-reap-stops-what-a-finished-agent-left` (the sweep that reaps what a finished agent left).
- **Retire.** Each loop and the root setup check the deadline before each tree. Idle is read only for trees that reach the decision that needs it. The sweep takes its kill limit from `session_end_sweep.py` and caps each subprocess timeout at the time left. Check: a case with 150 trees and slow git calls ends inside the kill limit. Each tree the deadline skipped is reported as not judged.
