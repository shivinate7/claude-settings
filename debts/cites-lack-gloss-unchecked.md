# No check requires a gloss on a cite

- **What.** Some cites of record ids, in decisions and debts, carry no gloss. No check requires one.
- **Where.** The `Debts:` lines and `Where` lines of records, such as `hold-unsafe-after-round-3`. The rule is `speak-cite-id-plus-gloss` in `CLAUDE.md` (cite an id with a short gloss).
- **Decision.** `hold-unsafe-after-round-3` (net-value hold after round 3).
- **Retire.** A check that reads each cite of a record id and goes red on one with no gloss.
