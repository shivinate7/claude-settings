# waiting-page-design

- New step, touches c100 (one waiting page for every repo), c99 (sessions read what a page saves) and the designer's d10 lanes: a **Designs tab** on each repo's waiting page. A question may carry `designs: [{slug, label}]`; its card shows the full mockup in a sandboxed frame beside it, and the tab pages through every design whose question is still open. Each mockup is stored whole in the page and leaves at the next republish once its question is answered, so mockups stop arriving as separate artifacts. Designers (d10) hand their mockup HTML to the page's owner session instead of publishing it. Limits checked Oct 26 22:58 CDT: 16 MB per page; a full mockup is about 52 KB, so about 300 fit. Other artifacts cannot be framed and the asset store refuses HTML, so inline is the only route.
