- New step, filed from shiv's message cmsg_01DoF7thf3iq2Srr1UsfXgh2U26shJXmnJbrCUvYLDun3h (degen waiting-page item 83, five degen PRs held about 2 hours). Title: "A small fix after the last review round gets one short read, then merges". Cause, checked on claude-settings main da8d8073: rule roles-review-cap-three (a change gets three review rounds) says "A change gets at most three review rounds" and that open findings become debts or notes, but says nothing about a fix landed after round 3. The merge grant (decision standing-merge-grant) still needs a fresh reviewer to approve the head that merges. A lane that fixes its round-3 finding (the better outcome) therefore lands on an unread head that no rule lets anyone read. Proposed rule, every repo: after the last round, a fix confined to that round's findings gets one "fix check": a fresh Sonnet read of the fix diff and the finding only. It is not a round, it can only pass or fail, and it cannot ask for more work. Pass merges on green. Fail holds the lane for the owner. A plain merge of main with no hand-edited conflict needs no read. Needs the owner's grant under outcomes-stale-decision-protocol before it lands.
