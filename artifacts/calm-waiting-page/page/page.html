Calm and uniform: one look in every repo

You are not cooked. Every repo's page is the same file published from one template, and all its questions and answers live in the page's own store, not in the page. So a new look ships by changing page.html once in claude-settings and republishing it to each repo's existing link. The link stays the same, and the open items and saved answers should stay with it, since the store belongs to the artifact and not to one version of the page. Prove that with one republish of the home app's page before the others. Today only the home app has a copy, so the fix costs one republish.

The direction

One quiet look everywhere, with one question in front of you at a time. The repo shows only as its name, at the top and in the browser tab. Everything else, from colours to type to where the buttons sit, is the same in every repo, so the habit you build answering home-app questions works on banchi the next night.

The choices, and why

  • One question at a time by default, with "All" one tap away. A long scroll of 22 cards asks you to hold the whole pile in your head. A single question with a quiet queue beside it shows how much is left without making you read it. "All" stays for the nights you want to sweep through fast.
  • Save moves you to the next open item, with Undo. No hunting for where you were. Undo shows at the bottom of the screen for five seconds, and Clear stays on the saved item.
  • "Take my recommendation" on each item, not one button for the whole page. One press per item keeps each save your word for that item only, which is how the skill already reads an answer. The page-wide "accept every pick" moves to the All view, where you can see what it covers.
  • The question leads, the reference follows. The current card opens with a number and a line of ids. Here the title is the largest text, then the context, then a muted "About" line that names the ids with their plain names.
  • Gain and lose sit side by side. You compare options by their trade, so the trade gets two short columns. "How it works" is one muted line above.
  • One typeface: Atkinson Hyperlegible. The Braille Institute drew it so similar letters (I, l, 1; O, 0) never blur. It has a matching mono for ids and numbers. One family is calmer than the current serif, sans and mono mix.
  • One accent, used only for "you can press this" and "this is my pick". Green means saved. Amber appears only when the page needs something from you, such as a note for "None of these". Nothing else carries colour.
  • Every item says where the answer goes, how long it has waited and, for a task, how long it takes. The data already holds home, added and minutes; the current page never shows them. Times are in CDT with the zone named.
  • Keys for the desk, 44-pixel targets for the phone. A, B, C choose; R takes the recommendation; Enter saves; J and K move. On a phone the queue folds into one "8 left" row above the question.

Should repo colours bleed in? No.

  • Colour already has a job here. It says "my pick", "saved" and "needs your note". A repo's brand colour would compete with those three meanings, and on a page you skim at midnight that costs you mistakes.
  • The habit is the product. You answer the same kind of question in nine repos. A page that looks the same everywhere lets your hands learn it once. A themed page feels like a new tool each time.
  • Nine palettes in two themes means 18 contrast checks, and a repo with no palette (home, the ops records) needs one invented for it. Uniform means one check.
  • The name does the identifying. Each repo has its own link, its own browser-tab title ("Waiting: home-app") and its name as the largest word in the header. Try the repo picker in the strip at the top: only the name changes, and it is enough.
  • If you ever want a cue anyway, a small coloured mark beside the name is the most I would allow, never in buttons or states. I would not build it until you find yourself on the wrong page.

What I would change in the template

All of these are in skills/waiting-page/page.html. Only the first touches the data contract in SKILL.md.

  1. Add a repo field to meta/page. The page puts it in the header and the tab title. Without it the page cannot name its repo, because the skill says to publish the page unchanged.
  2. Make "one at a time" the default view, with the queue, and keep a list view for sweeps.
  3. Move to the next open item after a save, with a five-second Undo that does the same as Clear.
  4. Add "Take my recommendation" to each decision. Move "accept every pick" into the list view and show the items it covers before it saves.
  5. Show home, the age from added, and a task's minutes.
  6. Format every time in America/Chicago, 24-hour, with CDT or CST.
  7. Swap the three fonts for Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Mono, at 16px body.
  8. Split the accent from state: one blue for action and pick, green for saved, amber for "needs you".
  9. Put gain and lose side by side, make "None of these" visibly quieter, and open the note box only on demand.
  10. Add the keys, and drop the fixed bottom bar.
  11. Keep "Copy answers as one message" in the list view. It is the recovery path when the store cannot be reached, so it must not depend on the store.

The page tests in skills/waiting-page/tests/ would need new cases for the auto-advance, per-item accept and CDT time format. Port to other repos: yes, by republishing to each repo's existing link, because every repo uses the same template.
