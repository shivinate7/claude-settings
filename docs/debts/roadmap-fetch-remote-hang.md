# A remote hang inside the roadmap fetch bound is unmeasured

- **What.** The roadmap fetch is bounded at 2 s. Its bound is not measured for real git-remote-https when the remote hangs. Under a hung fetch, one `past_rulings` read took 4.07 s, inside the 70 s budget of `call_judge`. `past_rulings` calls `fresh_ref` 2-3 times per read, and a failed fetch refetches on each call, so the bound can repeat within one read. Worst paths against their timeouts: `sendmessage_state` runs 47-49 s against its 50 s timeout. SessionStart's worst case is four 2 s bounds plus 2 s, which equals its 10 s timeout. Incident class: stale ref refusal (incident fb-0950-4, a refusal at 09:53 CST Nov 13). Harm: a hook that runs past its timeout is cut off, and the lane did not check what that does to its verdict.
- **Where.** `lint/roadmap_ref.py` (`FETCH_TIMEOUT`, `fetch_roadmap`). `hooks/question_form.py` (`past_rulings`, ~2060). `hooks/sendmessage_state.py` (the time budget comment, ~59-67). `hooks/session_start.sh` (`bounded_fetch`).
- **Decision.** Step `roadmap-ref-fresh-before-verdict` (the step whose round 3 review named these limits). Decision `ruled-store-one-home` (its freshness paragraph names this helper).
- **Retire.** Measure one `git fetch` of origin's roadmap against a remote that hangs, and record the wall time. A case in `hooks/test_sendmessage_state.py` shows the worst path stays under its 50 s timeout with a hung fetch. A case in `hooks/test_session_start.py` shows the start stays under its 10 s timeout with four hung fetches.
