# The Windows guard and merge shards grow with their suites

- **What.** Off pull_request, the guard suite runs in 2 Windows shards and the merge git-half suite in 5. From 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-06 the guard suite grew from 16800 to 28421 lines and the merge suite from 577 to 974 tests. A guard shard went from about 10 to about 16 min, and a merge shard from 5-9 to 11-14 min. Run 42282722326 ended checks-windows-merge-2 at its 15 min cap with no red test. The caps are now 29 (guard) and 22 (merge). The slowest green run is 69% (guard, 19.9 min) and 66% (merge, 14.5 min); the merge cap end, at least 15.2 min, is 69%. About 21% more growth in either suite puts its slowest shard past 80% of its cap. The suites grew 69% in those 10 days.
- **Where.** Jobs `checks-windows-guard`, `checks-windows-guard-2` and `checks-windows-merge` to `-5` in `.github/workflows/checks.yml`; the shard switch `GUARD_SHARD` in `hooks/test_guard.py` and `MERGE_SHARD` in `merge/test_merge.py`.
- **Decision.** `ci-job-caps` (each CI job's cap leaves its slowest run under 80%).
- **Retire.** A third Windows guard shard and a sixth Windows merge shard run on main, each a job of its own. Each has 5 green runs, read as "How to measure again" in decision `ci-job-caps` says.
