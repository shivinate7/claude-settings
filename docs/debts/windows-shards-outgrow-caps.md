# The guard and merge shards grow with their suites

- **What.** The guard suite runs in 3 Windows shards (on push, and on a ready pull_request since R62) (read job `checks-windows-guard` in `checks.yml`) and the merge git-half suite in 5 (read jobs `checks-windows-merge` to `checks-windows-merge-5` in `checks.yml`). From 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-06 the guard suite grew from 16800 to 28421 lines and the merge suite from 577 to 974 tests (measured 2026-11-11, git show e0f9e2d9 and 5e595822: wc -l of hooks/test_guard.py, grep -c "def test" of merge/test_merge.py). A merge shard went from 5-9 min (measured 2026-11-11, gh run view 40296937814: five gates-windows-merge jobs at 4.92 to 8.67 min) to a 16.0 to 17.6 min median (decision `ci-job-caps`, rows checks-windows-merge and checks-windows-merge-2 to -5, window of 2026-11-12). The 10.8 to 13.5 min medians of the window of 2026-11-06 are unmeasured now. They were in row checks-windows-merge-2 to -5 at commit e321af9e48, and commit 0274ca3bc6 replaced that row. Runs 42282722326 (merge-2) and 42437968376 (merge-5, on main) ended at the 15 min cap with no red test (measured 2026-11-11, gh run view 42282722326 and gh api attempt 1 of run 42437968376: both jobs cancelled at 15.18 min, no failed step). The merge cap is now 75 (interim stopgap, owner 12:11 CST Nov 10; `timeout-minutes` of the five merge jobs in `checks.yml`). It was 22 (decision `ci-job-caps`, row checks-windows-merge, column "Cap before"). The slowest green run of shards 2 to 5 in the window of 2026-11-06 was 14.5 min, 66% of 22 (row checks-windows-merge-2 to -5 at commit e321af9e48; unmeasured now). In the window of 2026-11-12 the slowest of the five merge shards is 21.2 min (checks-windows-merge, run 43155288101), 28% of 75 (decision `ci-job-caps`, rows checks-windows-merge and -2 to -5, column "Slowest as % of cap after"). The two cap ends on main, at 15.18 min each, are 69% of 22, the cap set after them (measured 2026-11-11, the two runs cited above; decision `ci-job-caps`, note "Cap ends after the window of 2026-11-06"). A 16% rise on the 15.18 min cap end would put a shard past 80% of 22. The guard split to 3 shards on main, at cap 20 each (measured 2026-11-11, git show 5fd384b86:.github/workflows/checks.yml: checks-windows-guard, -2 and -3 at timeout-minutes 20). Since then 11 main push runs (42700538152 to 43038567637, 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11) ended cancelled with a guard shard cut at the 20 min cap and no red test, so the 3-shard split did not hold: green shards took 15.27 to 19.98 min (measured 2026-11-11, gh run view on each of the 11 runs: a Windows guard shard cancelled at 20.05 to 20.08 min with no failed step; green shards 15.27 to 19.98 min). The three Windows guard caps are now 75 (`timeout-minutes` of jobs `checks-windows-guard`, `-2` and `-3` in `checks.yml`), a stopgap (ruling owner, 2026-11-10 12:11 CST, decision `ci-job-caps`). The cap no longer cuts a shard, but the shards still outgrow their work. Run 43113733476 (main, under the 75 min cap) had its three guard shards green at 16.88 to 23.58 min (measured 2026-11-11, gh run view 43113733476, commit c1c34562f). No fourth Windows guard shard is in checks.yml (measured 2026-11-11, grep checks-windows-guard-4 in .github/workflows/checks.yml: no match). The suites grew 69% in those 10 days (measured 2026-11-11, git show e0f9e2d9 and 5e595822: 16800 to 28421 lines, 577 to 974 tests). The guard suite kept growing: 37064 lines at commit a00301efe on 2026-11-09 (measured 2026-11-11, git show a00301efe:hooks/test_guard.py), and its Linux job median rose from 16.6 to 21.1 min (decision `ci-job-caps`, note "Window of 2026-11-09 (lane ci-guard-headroom)"). In batch 69 (PR 2461) run 42682869856 ended `checks-guard` attempt 1 at its 27 min cap with no red test (measured 2026-11-11, gh api attempt 1 of run 42682869856: checks-guard cancelled at 27.25 min, no failed step; PR 2461 head is 592fd2f3c, the run's commit, and its cap was 27 at that commit). So the Linux guard suite runs in 2 shards, `checks-guard` and `checks-guard-2`. Each has the provisional cap 60 (`timeout-minutes` of both jobs in `checks.yml`), and the per-shard time is unmeasured (decision `ci-job-caps`, table rows checks-guard and checks-guard-2).
- **Where.** Jobs `checks-windows-guard` to `-guard-3`, `checks-windows-merge` to `-5`, and `checks-guard` and `checks-guard-2` in `.github/workflows/checks.yml`; the shard switch `GUARD_SHARD` in `hooks/test_guard.py` and `MERGE_SHARD` in `merge/test_merge.py`.
- **Decision.** `ci-job-caps` (each CI job's cap leaves its slowest run under 80%).
- **Retire.** The debt retires when a sixth Windows merge shard runs on main as a job of its own. It also retires when each guard and merge shard, Linux and Windows, has 5 green runs under 80% of its cap. That pairing is this debt's own test. Its parts are in decision `ci-job-caps`. A new job keeps a provisional cap of 60 min until it has 5 green runs (decision `ci-job-caps`, 'How to measure again', step 6). That covers `checks-guard` and `checks-guard-2` only, and the 'Target met' column of their row says the same. The Windows shards hold the 75 min stopgap instead. The target is a slowest run under 80% of the cap (decision `ci-job-caps`, 'The ruling', the owner's 2026-10-27 cap rule, roadmap step c95).
