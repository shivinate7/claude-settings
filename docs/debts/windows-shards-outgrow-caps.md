# The guard and merge shards grow with their suites

- **What.** The Windows guard suite runs in 5 shards, each at the 75 min stopgap cap: shard 1 in job `checks-windows-guard`, which a ready pull request runs as its Windows slice, and shards 2 to 5 on a daily schedule and a manual dispatch only (decision `windows-prs-run-marked-tests`). The Windows merge git-half suite runs in 10 shards, each at 75 min: shard 1 in job `checks-windows-merge`, and shards 2 to 10 on a daily schedule and a manual dispatch only. The Linux guard suite runs in 4 shards (read .github/workflows/checks.yml:1417), each at the provisional cap 60, unmeasured per shard (read docs/decisions/ci-job-caps.md:37). On the 3-shard Windows guard split, green shards took 15.27 to 19.98 min, and 11 main push runs ended with a guard shard cut at its 20 min cap (read docs/decisions/ci-job-caps.md:69). The 5-shard and 10-shard splits are unmeasured.
- **Where.** Jobs `checks-windows-guard` to `-5`, `checks-windows-merge` to `-10`, and `checks-guard` to `-4` in `.github/workflows/checks.yml`; the shard switch `GUARD_SHARD` in `hooks/test_guard.py` and `MERGE_SHARD` in `merge/test_merge.py`.
- **Decision.** `ci-job-caps` (each CI job's cap leaves its slowest run under 80%) (read docs/decisions/ci-job-caps.md:1).
- **Retire.** The debt retires when a sixth Windows merge shard runs on main as a job of its own. It also retires when each guard and merge shard, Linux and Windows, has 5 green runs under 80% of its cap. The 'How to measure again' section of decision `ci-job-caps` sets the 5 green runs (read docs/decisions/ci-job-caps.md:90), and the 80% is its target (read docs/decisions/ci-job-caps.md:25).
