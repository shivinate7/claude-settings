# Ten readers of the roadmap, its record branch or main do not use the roadmap helper

- **What.** Nine readers read `origin/roadmap`, `origin/<record branch>` or `origin/<main branch>`, with no `fresh_ref` check, so they read the ref as the last fetch left it and say nothing about its age: `check_store` in `lint/ruled_store.py`; `step_names_template`, `export_check` and `Homes.filed_numbers` in `hooks/feedback_guard.py`; `_read_steps_of` in `hooks/question_form.py` (its caller `_read_steps` does check); `step_files` in `lint/check_artifact_cites.py`; `waiting_copy` in `lint/compliance_probes.py`; `skills/roadmap/build.py::record_source`, which reads the page's record branch with no fetch: the checkout's own tree when HEAD is on that branch, else `origin/<branch>`, else the local branch; `skills/roadmap/build.py::main_ref`, which only resolves the main ref (`origin/<branch>`, else the local branch) for the landed-marks read. A tenth, `fetch_branch` in `merge/merge.py`, runs its own `git fetch` of the branch with no time bound, outside `lint/roadmap_ref.py`. Incident class: stale ref refusal (incident fb-0950-4, a refusal at 09:53 CST Nov 13). Harm: a fix to the helper's bound or restore does not reach these readers, and a stale ref can refuse or pass a judged act with no note.
- **Where.** The ten functions above. `fresh_ref` in `lint/roadmap_ref.py` checks `origin/roadmap` only. `step_names_template`, `export_check`, `Homes.filed_numbers`, `step_files` and `waiting_copy` read the layout's record branch, which may have another name. `record_source` reads the record branch the page builder names. `main_ref` reads the main branch. A repo with another record branch needs a check that takes the branch name before any of these routes through the helper. The helper's docstring says no other file in `lint/` or `hooks/` runs a fetch of the roadmap branch. `lint/test_roadmap_ref.py` pins the seven unrouted readers in `UNROUTED_READERS`. Its scan reads `lint/` and `hooks/` only, and finds a reader by a literal ref name, so it does not pin the two `skills/roadmap/build.py` readers.
- **Decision.** Rule `building-one-home-per-capability` (give each capability one home, and call it from everywhere). Step `roadmap-ref-fresh-before-verdict` (the step that set the helper as the one fetch).
- **Retire.** Each reader either calls the helper (`fresh_ref`, or a check that takes the branch name) and moves from `UNROUTED_READERS` to `ROUTED_READERS` in `lint/test_roadmap_ref.py`, or a decision says why it reads the ref as it is. A case in `merge/test_merge.py` shows `fetch_branch` calls the helper and runs no fetch of its own, or a decision says why it keeps its own. The two `skills/roadmap/build.py` readers are outside that scan. A case in `skills/roadmap/test_build.py` must pin them, or a decision says why they read their ref as they are.
