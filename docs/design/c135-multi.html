Design choices (one per line; the pick is marked)
  1. Tick box. A fixed 26px square sits before the letter on each card of a multi item. Empty, it is an outline; ticked, it fills and shows a tick. Gain: the card reads as tick-any before a press; a radio card has no box. Loss: one more mark per card. Pick.
  2. Group hint. One line above the cards: "Tick any that apply." On a ranked item: "Tick any that apply, then put them in order, best first." It is the group's description. Gain: the owner knows before the first press. Loss: one line. Pick.
  3. Rank, option A (pick): the tick box shows the rank number in place of the tick. Option B: the box keeps the tick, and the rank row shows "1st of 3" beside the buttons. A gain: the number sits where the eye goes first, no new words. A loss: a number beside a letter. B gain: plain words. B loss: the number sits at the card's foot, away from the title. Hide either with its dashed button.
  4. Move-up and move-down buttons. Each card of a ranked item has a row with "Up" and "Down", 44px high and wide (a11y floor), in reading order after the card. The cards never move; only the numbers change (ux-order-holds-still). An unticked card keeps the row's space, hidden, so a tick moves nothing. At the top rank Up stays in place and focusable, marked off by a dashed border and a faded arrow, and says "Already first" on hover and focus; Down at the last rank says "Already last". Gain: focus never drops. Pick.
  5. A new tick goes last in the order. An untick closes the gap. Gain: no number jumps on the other cards. Pick.
  6. Card structure on a ranked item. A button cannot sit inside a label, so the card is a div. The label is the card's head and stretches over the card, so a press anywhere on the card still ticks it. The Up, Down and View controls sit above that layer. Owner grant: no; builder note.
  7. My pick on a list. Each picked card keeps the gold row. Pick-one form: the first picked card holds the reason, the others say "Also my pick". Ranked form: each says its place, "1st", "2nd", "3rd", and the 1st holds the reason. Gain: the reason shows once. Pick.
  8. Letters, not titles, in the ready line and on Take my pick: "A and B, your choice", "A, C, D, your order", "Take my pick (A + C)", "(C, A, D)". The ready line is one line, cut with an ellipsis; two titles do not fit it at 390, and "A, then C, then D" did not fit either. The letter sits on each card above. Pick.
  9. Saved card. "You chose:" or "You chose, in order:", then one row per pick: number when ranked, the letter, the title. Pick.
