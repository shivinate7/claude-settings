Empty box

Nothing typed and nothing stacked. Add to list and Send ask are off, and the hint says why.

One ask stacked

One press on Add to list moved the words out of the box into "Not sent yet". Nothing went to the store. Send ask sends that one.

Several asks stacked

Three asks wait in the list, each with Edit and Remove. The box holds a fourth, so Send counts four.

Edit a stacked ask

Edit opens the ask in place. It keeps its number and its place. Rows above it do not move.

Remove, with Undo

Remove keeps the row's place and offers Undo remove. The row goes at the next Add, Send or reload.

Final Send

The press is in flight. Send is off while the batch saves. Each row shows its own write. The box stays open for the next ask.

Sent, with one Undo for the batch

All three are saved under one time. One Undo at the box takes back the whole batch for 8 seconds.

Sent, no session told

The asks are saved, but no wake went out. The line says when the writer reads them.

Each ask's reach after send

Four minutes on. Each row has its own reach: one answered, one routed and read, one read and waiting.

Not read after 40 minutes

A batch that no session has read. Each row says how long, and offers Copy ask.

One ask not saved

Two of three saved. The third stays in the list with its words and its code. Send ask tries it again. Undo covers the two that saved.

Batch undone

Undo deleted the three records and put the asks back in the list, in their order. The box keeps what was typed since.
