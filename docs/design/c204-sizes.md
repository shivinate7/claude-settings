# Waiting page c204: a tall option picture scrolls in its box

Mockup: `c204-scroll.html`. It puts only its changes in `#newcss`, with today's value in a comment. Screen: `skills/waiting-page/page.html` at 1cc47f01 (`optPic`, `fitFrame`, `MIN_PIC`, `.dfitwrap`, `.opt .opic`). Owner's grant: waiting-page item 55 note, Oct 28 17:50 CDT. The mockup was measured with headless Chromium 153 at 390, 1280 and 2200, light and dark, and at 1065 (the narrowest page with three option columns). Fonts fall back offline.

1. When a picture scrolls. The scale does not change: sc = min(1, W / w, max(480 / h, 120 / w)), where w × h is the mockup (390 wide) or the focus box. The picture scrolls only when ceil(h × sc) > 481, so a pass of 1px or less counts as fitting and the box is min(480, ceil(h × sc)), with no scroll and no cue. That is a box more than 4 times as tall as it is wide, such as a 390-wide mockup taller than 1563px (a 1561px mockup draws 480 high, cut by 1px). A focus box under 120 wide and over 481 high also scrolls. Every other picture draws as today: the same width and transform, and the same height up to the 480 cap.
2. Question 1, a focused card. Pick: 1A.
   - 1A (state `focus-in`): the card scrolls only over the focus box, and opens at the top of the box. Gain: this keeps today's rule, that the card shows only the focus element (`SKILL.md`, the `focus` bullet; `fitFrame` translates by −box). The open position is scrollTop 0, so no timing can make it wrong. A focus that fits is unchanged. Loss: the mockup above and below the focus is still only in View.
   - 1B (state `focus-whole`): the card scrolls the whole mockup in the focus column, and opens with the focus at the top (scrollTop = round(box.y × sc)). Gain: the context round the focus is in the card. Loss: the open position needs the box that the frame posts, so a cached or declared first paint opens in the wrong place. A focus narrower than the mockup cuts the columns at its sides.
   - Departs from (1B only): `SKILL.md` "the option card then shows that element whole", and the `fitFrame` focus translate. Owner grant: yes, only if the owner wants 1B. Not blocking. "Hide 1A" and "Hide 1B" each remove one option.
3. Question 2, the open position and the cue. Pick: the pill cue.
   - Open position: the top of what the card shows. That is the top of the mockup with no focus, and the top of the focus box with a focus.
   - The pill: "↓ More", centred 8px above the bottom edge. It is 24px high with padding 0 8, radius 12, and 600 12px text. Its colours are `--pic-cue` #161b22 and `--on-pic-cue` #ffffff, with a 1px ring in `--on-pic-cue`. It sits on a 40px shade, rgb(22 27 34 / 0.28) at the bottom edge. A 12px shade at the top edge shows once the picture is scrolled. The cue goes when the picture reaches its end (scrollTop + clientHeight ≥ scrollHeight − 1).
   - Contrast, measured: the pill on the frame is 17.30:1, and the text on the pill is 17.30:1. The frame is #fff in both themes, so the values hold in light and in dark. The cue never sits on `--surface`.
   - The cue and the shades are sticky overlays whose net height is 0, so they move nothing. Their opacity fades in 150ms linear, only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
   - Gain: the pill shows on any mockup colour and on every platform. Loss: it covers about 64 × 24px of the picture's last visible row until the end.
   - Not picked: the scrollbar alone. Overlay scrollbars on iOS, Android and macOS hide when idle, so a phone shows no cue. Not picked: a line under the picture, such as "Scrolls". It covers nothing, but it is far from the edge where the content goes on, and it adds a row to every card in the row.
4. Question 3, tap or scroll on a phone. Pick: the native split. The frame stays inert (pointer-events none). A drag on the picture scrolls the box, and a tap opens View, as today.
   - Measured with touch at 390: a drag scrolled the picture from 0 to its end (261px) and opened nothing. A tap opened View once. The same drag on a plain control scroller scrolled 242px and fired no click.
   - Keep the default `overscroll-behavior`. At the picture's end, a drag or a wheel moves the page.
   - Gain: no gesture code. Loss: the page scroll pauses while the picture under the finger or the wheel scrolls.
   - A press on the scrollbar fires `click` on `.opic`. The mockup measured this for both the thumb and the track. So `open()` ignores a press whose clientX is past the wrap's left + clientLeft + clientWidth. Measured: a press on the gutter opened View 0 times, and a press on the picture opened it 1 time.
5. Question 4, the keyboard path. Pick: K1. The View sheet stays the keyboard route to the whole mockup.
   - K1: `.opic` stays the one tab stop of the picture. While it has focus, ArrowUp and ArrowDown scroll 40px, and PageUp and PageDown scroll clientHeight − 40. Home and End go to the ends. Call preventDefault only when scrollTop changed, so at an end the page scrolls. Enter and Space still open View. In the scroll case, the aria-label adds ". Arrow keys scroll its picture". Measured: ArrowDown twice moved 80px. Gain: one tab stop per card, as today. Loss: a screen reader's browse mode takes the arrow keys, so its user reads the mockup in View.
   - K2 (not picked): the picture becomes its own focusable scroll region, and View becomes a real `<button>`. Gain: arrow keys scroll the region with no script. Loss: two tab stops on each scroll card, and the picture is no longer part of the button. Departs from: `optPic`'s one `role=button` round the picture and View.
   - Both: the wrap gets tabindex −1 and `aria-hidden="true"`. The card's iframe gets `inert`.
   - Why, measured on a copy of the card's DOM shape (a `role=button` > wrap > sizer > sandboxed iframe that holds a button), not on page.html itself: Chromium makes a scroller with no focusable child a tab stop. A tab stop in a `role=button` is an axe `nested-interactive` violation. With tabindex −1 only, axe reports `scrollable-region-focusable`. With `aria-hidden` added, `@axe-core/playwright` 4.10 (which runs axe in each frame) reports 0 violations, with the iframe as it is, with tabindex −1, or with `inert`. `aria-hidden` is true to the content: a button's children are presentational, its label names the option, and View holds the same mockup.
   - Why `inert` on the iframe: in that copy, Tab went radio, picture, into the frame, then the next control. With tabindex −1 or `inert`, Tab skips the frame. `inert` also makes the frame's document inert, so no browser tabs into it. Departs from: today's tab order, where the frame is a stop. This is a fix, so no grant is needed.

7. Builder rules.
   - The change applies only to `optPic`'s call (maxH 480). `designPreview` and `stageFrame` do not change.
   - DOM of a capped wrap: wrap > `.dtop`, `.dsizer` > iframe, `.dcue` > `.dmore`. `.dsizer` is display block, position relative, overflow hidden, and pw × ceil(bh × sc), where bh is the focus box height, else the mockup height. The iframe keeps today's position and transform inside the sizer.
   - Why a sizer: a scroller's range starts at 0, so scroll overflow cannot reach negative translate coordinates. The scroll case needs a sized inner box plus scrollTop. Also, a transformed iframe adds its whole box to the scroll range unless a box with overflow hidden clips it. With 1B, the sizer is ceil(ht × sc) high, and only x is translated.
   - In `fit()`, compute sc as today, then set scroll = ceil(bh × sc) > maxH, and toggle `.scroll`. In the scroll case, read g = wrap.offsetWidth − wrap.clientWidth − 2 after `.scroll` is on. g is the gutter plus any border past 1px, such as the 3px left border of a `.dif` picture (measured: g 12 there). Set the wrap to (pw + 2 + g) × maxH. Do this in both branches, the measured one and the declared-size one, so the first paint is already 480 high. The declared branch then sets the height and no aspect-ratio.
   - With 5B, the width budget is floor(column rect width) − 2 − g. A rounded clientWidth let the box pass the column by 0.17px.
   - Set the first scrollTop once per wrap, never inside `fit()`, because `fit()` runs on every resize, message and repaint. With 1A, the first scrollTop is 0, so there is nothing to set. With 1B, set it after the first fit with a measured box (in the message handler), behind a per-wrap flag. When the person has scrolled, skip it.
   - The repaint merge in `syncOpts()` swaps only `.view` and keeps the wrap, so the scroll position survives a repaint. The builder confirms this.
   - A passive scroll listener on the wrap toggles `.scrolled` and `.at-end`. Call it once after each fit too.
   - The gutter: `.dfitwrap.scroll` has `scrollbar-width: thin` and `scrollbar-gutter: stable`. Headless Chromium draws a default scrollbar as an overlay (0px, measured). With `thin`, it draws a 10px classic gutter, so the measurements cover the classic case.
   - Acceptance check for "a picture that fits looks as today": load page.html at 1cc47f01 and at the build head with the same designs. For every picture that fits, the wrap's width and height and the iframe's transform are equal.
