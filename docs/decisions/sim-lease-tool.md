# bin/sim-lease is the one simulator queue and lease on a Mac

## The rulings

One tool, `bin/sim-lease`, holds the Mac-wide queue and lease for phone simulators. It replaces the Mac sim gatekeeper session as the thing that grants a boot. The gatekeeper (a session) reads `sim-lease status` and need not stay awake. The mistake it stops: a session boots a phone simulator outside the gatekeeper's one queue, or over the load bars.

1. Owner rulings, degen `shared-sim-lease`, 2026-11-02 13:49 to 14:47 CST, as `skills/ios-apps/SKILL.md` ("Sharing the Mac") cites them. One phone simulator boots at a time. The two Duos may run together when the load allows. The 18 Pro never runs beside a Duo. A boot needs a 5-minute load under 100. Slot 2 needs a 5-minute load under 60 and slot 1 in use. Each boot needs a 1-minute load under 60. The next in line takes the first slot to free.
2. `sim-lease take --device duo|pro --pid PID [--udid]` joins one strict FIFO queue and waits for its turn. Only the head is granted. It prints the slot (`1`, `2` or `pro`), or with `--udid` that slot's device UDID from `SLOT_UDID`, and exits 0. At the wait limit (`SIM_LEASE_WAIT`, default 300 s) it exits 1 with one stderr line that names the holders and the load. `--pid` is required on `take` and `give`; a usage error, a missing `--pid` included, prints one usage line on stderr and exits 2.
   - `take --for-udid UDID` (coordinator ruling, 2026-11-08 12:17 CST, option A; slot 2 and the wait by lane picks, 12:36 and 12:38 CST) is for a device that is already booted. It grants the slot whose `SLOT_UDID` is UDID, matched without case, and prints the slot. It obeys the FIFO queue, the slot's holder and the pro/duo exclusion, and waits for them as a plain `take` does, up to the wait. It skips the load bars and reads no load, since it boots nothing. Slot 2 is granted for its UDID even while slot 1 is free. A UDID in no slot, or in a slot of the other role (`pro` takes only the pro slot, `duo` only slot 1 or 2): one stderr line, exit 2, judged before the no-op. Not granted in time: exit 1 with one stderr line that names each holder's pid and session.
3. Limits on every grant, except for `take --for-udid` (ruling 2): 5-minute load under 100, 1-minute load under 60. Duo takes slot 1, or slot 2 only while slot 1 is held and the 5-minute load is under 60. Pro is exclusive both ways. The load is read from `sysctl -n vm.loadavg` only.
4. `bin/sim-test` takes the lease for its role (`--device duo` by default, else `pro`) on its own-boot path. It boots the UDID from `take --udid`. It takes the lease after its lock and the cap check and before `simctl boot`. It gives the lease back after the shutdown and before it frees its lock, on every exit, INT and TERM too. A reused device takes `take --for-udid` for its own UDID and holds that lease for the whole run or hold. So a lane's later MCP calls on it pass the device rule (`sim-test-is-the-one-boot-path`, ruling 5). When the scan after a grant finds a reusable device, the granted slot goes back first. A reused device in no slot, or a `none` under CI: one stderr line, and the run goes on with no lease (lane picks, 12:36 CST). A `--for-udid` take not granted in time, because another live holder keeps the slot (coordinator ruling, 12:37 CST): exit 1, nothing booted, nothing run, and the line names the holder. A `--hold` run keeps its lease for the whole hold.
5. Recovery does not depend on the holder ending well (`verification-recovery-independent-of-state`). Each call reaps a lease or a waiter whose pid is dead, or whose `ps -o lstart=` start time differs from the one recorded at take (a reused pid). `ps` runs with `TZ=UTC`, so a start time does not change with the caller's `TZ`. A holder that dies never has to give.
6. State: `<config>/state/sim-lease/leases.json`, under `fcntl.flock` on `lock` in the same folder, written through a temp file and `os.replace`. `<config>` is `$CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, else `~/.claude`.
7. No-op: when `uname -s` is not Darwin, or `CI` or `GITHUB_ACTIONS` equals `true`, `take` prints `none` (with `--udid` or `--for-udid` too) and exits 0, `give` exits 0, and no file is written. On Darwin, a no-op `take` also prints one stderr line saying no lease was taken. Any other value, `false` or empty, is not a no-op. So CI runs and Linux hosts are never queued.
8. Cost: per call, one `uname` and one `ps` per recorded pid. Take and status add one `sysctl`; a waiting take adds one `sysctl` and one `ps` per recorded pid each `SIM_LEASE_POLL` seconds (default 2). A `take --for-udid` reads no load, so it adds no `sysctl`. A reuse in `bin/sim-test` adds one `take` and one `give` process. It writes `leases.json` only when the content changes, plus the empty `lock` file. No daemon, no timer, no log.

Test: `lint/test_sim_lease.py` (slots, load bars, queue order, reaping by dead pid and by changed start time, give, usage, no-op, concurrent takes) and the `take --for-udid` cases of the same file, and cases 47 to 50 and 81 to 87 of `lint/test_sim_test.sh` (lease held at boot, released after the run and after TERM, refusal at load 100 boots nothing, a reuse holds its own slot's lease and gives it back, a device in no slot runs with no lease). No test covers a `--for-udid` take that times out in `bin/sim-test` (exit 1 naming the holder). Both use shims for `uname`, `sysctl` and `ps`.

Not proven here: there is no Mac in the cloud. The real `sysctl -n vm.loadavg` text, the real `ps -o lstart=` format on macOS and a real `simctl` boot under the lease are untested here. The shims write those formats as the tool expects them. The shell-macos CI job runs the sim-test suite on a Mac, but with shimmed load and `ps`.

Not covered: a SIGKILL of `sim-test` skips its EXIT trap, so its simulator (a Duo or the 18 Pro) stays booted while its lease is reaped. The next `sim-test` run reads the booted list after its grant: the same role reuses the device, the other role refuses (debt note `sim-test-sigkill-leaves-sim-booted`). An `xcodebuild -destination` boot skips the lease, and the route rule refuses it (decision `sim-test-is-the-one-boot-path`, ruling 5). A simulator MCP call on a device is allowed only to a session that holds a live lease for that device (rule `simulator-device-lease`). The guard reads `leases.json` through `live_leases` and `SLOT_UDID` in `bin/sim-lease`, and names the role in its refusal from `SLOT_UDID`.

Basis: owner rulings (degen `shared-sim-lease`, 2026-11-02 13:49 to 14:47 CST), and the standing need that a session never boots over the load bars. Landed: 2026-11-04.
