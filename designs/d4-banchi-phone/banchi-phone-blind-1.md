## Job
Two people do the job. They share one store of trading card singles.

The seller, in their own words: "Bulk-list pre-sorted TCG singles on TCGplayer with zero attention per card, and know where every card physically is." The seller sorts cards into boxes by hand, in a known order, before any phone work. They hold a stack of cards and a box. They photograph each card in order, one after another, and want to give each card no thought. A machine reads each photograph and names the card, its set, number and printing. A card it cannot name for certain waits for the seller, with its photograph. Nothing is guessed. The seller then sends the named cards to TCGplayer as listings, at a price, in one send. Done for the seller: every card in the box is listed or knowingly held back, and the phone can say which box and which place in the box each card sits in.

The helper is a retired family member, not technical. When a card sells, the helper must find it. They hold the phone and stand at the shelf of boxes. They must find out which box to open and which card to pull, then mark it pulled and packed. Done for the helper: each sold card is in its envelope, and nothing is left to pull. Their screens must explain themselves.

The things the job handles:
- A card: name, set, number, printing or finish, condition, a photograph, a price, and a state (waiting to be named, named, waiting on the seller, listed, sold, pulled).
- A box: a name the seller gives it, and an ordered set of places. Dividers split a box into sections.
- A place: the card's position in its box, counted by cards, not by slots. When a card leaves, the cards after it move up one.
- A sitting: one photographing session. A busy sitting holds about 300 cards.
- A send: one batch of listings to TCGplayer. Prices come from a market reading. Cards under a price floor (about $0.49) are held back.
- An order: a buyer, an order number, one or more cards, each in some box. A busy day holds 10 to 40 orders.
- A store holds about 30 boxes and some thousands of cards, across a few games.
