Sample. No figure, name, price, order or place on this page is real.

Lane kind: blind. Lane phone-blind-1. Three concepts for one job: list a pre-sorted stack of trading card singles on TCGplayer with no per-card typing, and know the box and slot of every card. Each part is marked with a dashed "New:" label: a blind lane sees no current screen, so no part is marked "Today:".

Device: iPhone Duo, folded (outer screen), then iPhone Duo, unfolded (inner screen). 1 CSS px = 1 pt. Hatched areas are the trailing inset and the fold strip: no text or control sits on them.

C1 · Stack run

Task first. A stack is the unit of work. Scan the stack, look only at the cards the app is not sure of, then list the whole stack with one press. Each card gets the next slot of the box in stack order. Tabs: Stacks, Orders, Places. First screen: Stacks.

C2 · Shelf

Place first. The first screen is the person's boxes, drawn as they sit on the table: each box a bar of slots, each card a sliver coloured by its listing state. A stack pours into the next free slots, and a sale lights its slivers. Tabs: Shelf, Orders, and Search. First screen: Shelf.

C3 · Only what needs you (bold)

No card list and no tab bar. The pose is the way to move: folded, the phone is a scanner in the hand that shows one count and where the stack goes; unfolded on the table, it is a desk that shows only the cards the app is not sure of, the orders to pull, and a "Where is" field. Every sure card is listed as it is read. First screen folded: the camera. First screen unfolded: Needs you.
