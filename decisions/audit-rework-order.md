# Audit findings are reworked in a fixed order

Owner ruling, 2026-10-16 10:50 CDT. An audit lane works its findings in this order:

1. Delete copies.
2. Merge overlapping entries into tighter lists.
3. Turn number checks into result checks.
4. Fix code.

A prose mandate stays in its central home. Every other record cites it by id and gloss.

The owner gave the order and no reason. Reason not recorded.

## Terms, by the orchestrator's call

The ruling does not define three terms. A lane reads them this way:

- Copy: a second statement of a fact that another entry or rule already holds. Step 1 keeps
  the home that code or a check reads, and deletes the copy.
- Number check: a check that passes or fails on a count (rules, lines, bytes). A result check
  passes or fails on the outcome the count stood for.
- Central home: the CLAUDE.md rule, or the one decision, that owns the question.

## Where it lives

The order has no check. In CLAUDE.md, `building-one-home-per-capability` backs step 1 only
(delete copies). Nothing in CLAUDE.md backs steps 2 to 4, the order, or the central-home
clause. `outcomes-judge-by-outcome` is the nearest general rule and states no step. Both Home
choices are the orchestrator's call.
